# vision-strategy-align

Verifies that a repo's two grounding documents work together as one source of truth:

- **`VISION.md`** — operator-authored, canonical product definition. What the product is and why it must exist.
- **`STRATEGY.md`** — derived strategic framing (typically generated by the CE plugin's `ce-strategy` skill). How we win and where we invest.

**Precedence rule: VISION.md governs.** STRATEGY.md is a derivation, never a second source of truth. When they conflict, the strategy is wrong until the operator says otherwise.

## When to use

- After `VISION.md` or `STRATEGY.md` is created or edited
- After running `ce-strategy` (create or update)
- Before brainstorming or planning begins, if both documents exist
- Whenever you're asked to "check alignment", "check drift", or "are vision and strategy in sync"

## What it does

1. **Locates the documents** — handles missing-document cases (only VISION, only STRATEGY, neither).
2. **Runs a six-check alignment rubric** — derivation header, traceability, no new scope, no contradiction, ownership, and freshness. Each check passes or fails with evidence (quote or line reference).
3. **Reports** a verdict: **aligned**, **drifted**, or **conflicted**. It never silently edits either document — fixes apply only with operator approval, and STRATEGY.md is fixed to match VISION.md, not the reverse.
4. **Verifies the repo's agent instructions** (AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md) carry the precedence convention and a check trigger, offering to add them if missing.

## Why it exists

The CE plugin's `ce-strategy` skill has no concept of an upstream vision document and never reads `VISION.md`. CE's downstream skills consume STRATEGY.md directly (`ce-plan`, `ce-brainstorm`, `ce-ideate`, `ce-dogfood`, `ce-product-pulse`), so a drifted STRATEGY.md silently poisons planning, ideation, and QA. This skill makes vision→strategy consistency deliberate.

## Boundaries

- Checks and reports only; edits documents only with explicit operator approval.
- Does not author visions (operator's job) or strategies (`ce-strategy`'s job).
- Does not review general document quality — use `ce-doc-review` or `clarity-without-drift` for that.
