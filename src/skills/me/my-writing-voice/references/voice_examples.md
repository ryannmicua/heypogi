# Ryann Writing Voice Examples

Use these examples to match Ryann's voice across common writing tasks. Examples are sanitized and pattern-based, not source-of-truth records.

## 1. Senior leader: project hold / status clarification

### Draft in Ryann's voice

```text
Dear Sir Allan,

Please know that since last year, given the directions provided, the project has already been tabled indefinitely on our end and we have already informed our vendors of this status.

Just let us know when you'd like to take up this project again.

Thanks.
```

### Pattern notes

- Opens respectfully.
- States status directly.
- Mentions action already taken.
- Leaves the decision point with the leader.
- No defensive explanation.

## 2. Senior leader: upcoming renewal / approval awareness

### Draft in Ryann's voice

```text
Hi Sir Allan,

Want to let you know about the upcoming renewal: [proposal/link]

1. Barracuda Firewall License - expires on September 2026
- Cost: PHP 450k for 1YR, PHP 1.3M for 3YRs
- Firewall appliance deployed in LHC building. Serves SunPlus, GCAS, LHC Hotel, and partly convention hall internet
- Could discuss passing on charges to GCP with Kelo
```

### Pattern notes

- Starts with awareness, not a long introduction.
- Uses a numbered item for the renewal.
- Provides cost, operational purpose, and possible chargeback path.
- Keeps recommendation implicit unless a decision is required.

## 3. Staff feedback: improving a work artifact

### Draft in Ryann's voice

```text
Overall, the document will most likely pass the client's expectations.

However, for completeness, please add the following:

1. Basis for the cost of each line item.
   I understand a link to the pricing page is provided. However, this requires me to infer pricing level, unit of measure, and unit of time in order to come up with the estimated cost. Please don't make the reader do research.

2. Use simpler business language.

Original:
[original text]

Business language:
[replacement text]
```

### Pattern notes

- Begins with a practical assessment.
- Uses `However, for completeness...` to transition into corrections.
- Explains why the correction matters.
- Gives a replacement rather than only criticism.
- The phrase `Please don't make the reader do research` is very characteristic.

## 4. Staff feedback: cost table / executive summary

### Draft in Ryann's voice

```text
1. Present only one amount. Do not include the 2-year domain expense since this is already paid for. Also helps lower the requested amount.

2. Rename "contingency" to "1st year operating reserve" and use actual amount of $300. Standard contingency is 5-10%. 50% is too high as a contingency.

Here's a simple table you can use in the executive summary:

2026 Operating Forecast

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Base Annual OpEx | 553.99 | Covers hosting, database, website domain, platform usage, email services |
| 1st Year Operating Reserve | 300.00 | Because there is no prior-year operating history, the reserve helps address uncertainty in first-year usage and cost patterns. |
| TOTAL | 853.99 | |

This forecast assumes XX monthly active users, and XX monthly requests.
```

### Pattern notes

- Direct corrections first.
- Rationale is short and business-oriented.
- Replacement table is ready to paste.
- `XX` placeholders are acceptable when the recipient needs to fill in assumptions.

## Rewrite examples

### Inflated source

```text
I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to respectfully request your kind assistance in preparing certificates of completion for our student interns who have successfully concluded their required training hours with our department.
```

### Ryann-style rewrite

```text
Hi Ate Nely,

May I request certificates of completion for our OJTs who completed their required hours with us?

Here are their names:
- [name]
- [name]
- [name]
```

## Executive language rewrite

### Too abstract

```text
This artifact presents the initial annual operating platform/vendor cost needed to run the LHC Guest Booking System. The recommended approval baseline is the Minimum Practical Cost option, excluding the optional AI recommender module.
```

### Ryann-style business language

```text
This document shows the estimated first-year platform and vendor costs needed to run the LHC Guest Booking System.

The recommended approval amount is based on the Minimum Practical Cost option, which does not include the optional AI recommender module.
```

## Decision checklist before finalizing a draft

1. Is the ask or conclusion in the first paragraph?
2. Can the recipient act without asking for missing details?
3. Are dates, amounts, names, IDs, links, and owners included when available?
4. Can any paragraph become bullets or a small table?
5. Did the draft avoid generic corporate opening and closing lines?
6. Does the tone match the recipient relationship?
7. Is it shorter than a normal assistant-written version?
